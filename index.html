About

What I do, and the part of a project I own.

ASEAN Engineer ASEAN Eng Professional Mechanical Engineer SCE Registered Mechanical Engineer PRC Lean Six Sigma Green Belt LSSGB LEED Green Associate 2017–2019 PMP training completed

Twenty years of comprehensive EPC and FEED experience. My career covers the entire project lifecycle — Front-End Engineering Design and FEED verification, detailed engineering, procurement support, package management, construction, and pre-commissioning and commissioning site support. Onshore and offshore.

I specialise as a Senior Rotary / Mechanical Package Engineer, holding sole discipline responsibility for multi-million dollar rotating equipment packages delivered for global majors including Saudi Aramco and Santos Ltd, alongside Shell, Marathon Petroleum, Dow Chemical and ADNOC's Borouge.

My work centres on the package procurement cycle: generating technical specifications and mechanical datasheets, raising Material Requisitions, leading Technical Bid Evaluations, and executing thorough Vendor Document Reviews. On site I have supported pre-commissioning and commissioning, witnessed Factory Acceptance Testing, and participated in Joint Engineering Inspection & Testing prior to client turnover.

I work to API 610, 613, 614, 616, 617, 619, 670, 671 and 682, alongside ASME, TEMA and the relevant NFPA codes — and I manage the multi-discipline interfaces where mechanical packages meet process, electrical, instrumentation and civil scope. I currently work in Qatar as a Senior Mechanical Engineer with project management responsibility on high-value construction projects.
